My Love from the Star: 1-Night Filming Location Itinerary

Language: English | Category: K-Travel

Namsan Tower · Petite France · Han River — Walk in Do Min-joon and Cheon Song-yi's Footsteps

Photo by Taryn Elliott / Pexels

When My Love from the Star (별에서 온 그대) aired in 2013, the chemistry between Jun Ji-hyun and Kim Soo-hyun sparked a Hallyu wave that swept across Asia and beyond. The romance between Do Min-joon, a 400-year-old alien stranded on Earth, and top actress Cheon Song-yi produced countless iconic scenes — and the real-world locations where they were filmed remain must-visit destinations for Korean drama fans today.

This guide maps out a 1-night itinerary covering three key filming locations: Petite France in Gapyeong, N Seoul Tower on Namsan, and Han River Park. Visit during cherry blossom season for the full drama-come-to-life experience.

Stop 1: Petite France, Gapyeong — Do Min-joon's Rooftop

The iconic rooftop scene where Do Min-joon stands gazing into the distance while using his telekinetic powers was filmed at Petite France, a French-themed cultural village nestled in the hills of Gapyeong County, about an hour east of Seoul. The colorful Provencal-style buildings, European gardens, and winding stone paths give the park a dreamlike quality that perfectly matched the drama's otherworldly atmosphere.

Beyond the filming connection, Petite France offers a Little Prince-themed museum, a music box gallery, and French puppet shows. In spring, tulips and cherry blossoms frame the pastel buildings, making it one of the most photogenic spots in Gyeonggi Province. The park is accessible via the Gapyeong tourist shuttle bus from Gapyeong Station in about 30 minutes.

Petite France Visitor Information
Address: 1063 Hoban-ro, Cheongpyeong-myeon, Gapyeong-gun, Gyeonggi-do
Getting there: Gapyeong Station → Gapyeong Tourist Shuttle Bus (~30 min)
Hours: Please verify — hours vary by season; check the Gapyeong County official site
Source: Korea Tourism Organization, Gapyeong County official

Stop 2: N Seoul Tower — Romance with a View

Photo by Theodore Nguyen / Pexels

Perched atop Namsan mountain, N Seoul Tower is Seoul's most recognizable landmark and a backdrop for countless K-Drama love scenes — My Love from the Star among them. The emotional moments between Do Min-joon and Cheon Song-yi set against Seoul's glittering nightscape are etched in the memories of fans worldwide.

Take the Namsan Cable Car for a scenic ascent, then head to the observation deck for a 360-degree panorama of the capital. The view is stunning in daylight, but arriving around sunset lets you watch the city transform from golden glow to a sea of twinkling lights. Don't miss the love lock trees surrounding the tower, where couples attach padlocks as tokens of their affection — a tradition amplified by the K-Drama tourism boom.

N Seoul Tower Visitor Information
Address: 105 Namsangongwon-gil, Yongsan-gu, Seoul
Getting there: Myeongdong Station → Namsan Shuttle Bus or Cable Car
Hours: Please verify — check the Korea Tourism Organization official site
Source: Korea Tourism Organization official

Stop 3: Han River Park — The Chimaek First-Snow Scene

Photo by Sangmin Jeong / Pexels

"When the first snow falls, you have to have chimaek!" — Cheon Song-yi's famous line didn't just create a meme; it turned fried chicken and beer by the Han River into a pilgrimage for drama fans from around the world. After the show aired, chimaek sales in China reportedly surged, and Han River Park picnics became synonymous with the K-Drama lifestyle.

Yeouido Hangang Park and Banpo Hangang Park are the top picks for spring picnics. Spread a mat on the grassy banks, order fried chicken via a delivery app to a designated pickup point, and enjoy the view. For extra adventure, rent a bicycle and ride along the riverside path, or hop on a cruise boat for skyline views from the water. After sunset, the Banpo Bridge Rainbow Fountain light show adds a spectacular finale to the day.

Han River Park Visitor Information
Location: Yeouido Hangang Park or Banpo Hangang Park
Getting there: Yeouinaru Station (Line 5) or Express Bus Terminal Station (Lines 3, 7, 9)
Hours: Open 24 hours (some facilities have limited hours)
Source: Korea Tourism Organization official

Suggested 1-Night Itinerary

  • Morning: Seoul → Gapyeong via ITX-Cheongchun train (~1 hour); explore Petite France
  • Lunch: Gapyeong dakgalbi (spicy stir-fried chicken) street
  • Afternoon: Return to Seoul → Namsan Cable Car → N Seoul Tower observation deck
  • Evening: Watch the sunset from Namsan, then head to Han River Park
  • Night: Chimaek picnic at Han River Park — recreate the iconic first-snow scene

Travel Tips

  • Cherry blossom season (late March to mid-April) is the best time for this itinerary — the pink blooms transform every stop.
  • Book ITX-Cheongchun tickets to Gapyeong in advance, especially on weekends.
  • For Han River chimaek, use Korean delivery apps (Baemin, Coupang Eats) and collect at designated park pickup spots.
  • Weekday afternoons are quieter for Namsan Tower visits.
