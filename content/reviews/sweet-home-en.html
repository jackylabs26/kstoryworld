Plot Summary

Sweet Home presents a chilling premise that explores the darkest aspects of human nature when civilization crumbles. The story unfolds in a world where an unprecedented catastrophe transforms ordinary people into monstrous creatures driven by primal instincts and insatiable hunger. The narrative focuses on a resilient group of survivors living in a high-rise residential building who must navigate the terrifying new reality where the line between human and monster becomes increasingly blurred. These survivors find themselves trapped in an isolated community, forced to depend on one another for survival while dealing with internal conflicts, moral dilemmas, and the constant threat of transformation. The series masterfully explores what happens when people are pushed to their absolute limits—how they respond to fear, how they protect their loved ones, and whether they can maintain their humanity in the face of extinction. Throughout the episodes, the drama delves deep into psychological warfare, the formation of unexpected bonds, and the struggle to preserve hope in a seemingly hopeless world. The plot never becomes predictable, as each season introduces new challenges, mysterious elements, and shocking revelations that keep viewers on the edge of their seats. The writers have crafted a narrative that works both as a thrilling action-adventure and as a thoughtful meditation on human nature, survival instincts, and what truly defines our humanity.
