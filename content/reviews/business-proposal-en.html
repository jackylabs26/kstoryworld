Overview

Business Proposal is a South Korean romantic comedy drama that premiered on February 28, 2022, and quickly became a beloved addition to the K-drama landscape. With a perfect 8.428 out of 10 rating on TMDB, this series demonstrates the power of smart writing, excellent casting, and charming performances. The drama consists of 12 tightly-paced episodes in its first season, each running approximately one hour, making it an easily digestible yet thoroughly entertaining viewing experience. The combination of comedy and drama genres creates a perfect balance that appeals to a wide audience, from seasoned K-drama fans to newcomers looking for their next favorite series. The premise is simple yet compelling: what happens when a case of mistaken identity leads to an unexpected workplace romance with the ultimate power imbalance? The answer, as Business Proposal demonstrates, can be surprisingly heartwarming, hilarious, and deeply satisfying.

Plot Summary

At the heart of Business Proposal lies a brilliantly executed premise that serves as the perfect setup for romantic comedy gold. Shin Ha-ri, a talented and intelligent employee at a prestigious company, is facing an unexpected crisis in her personal life. Her colleague and friend are arranging a blind date for her, despite her repeated protests. In a moment of desperation, Ha-ri devises a plan to scare away the prospective suitor by showing up in disguise as her friend instead. This scheme is designed to be foolproof – she'll act so ridiculously and inappropriately that the man will have no interest in pursuing any relationship.

However, fate has other plans. The blind date turns out to be none other than Kang Tae-moo, the cold and unapproachable CEO of her own company. Not only is Ha-ri's cover blown when Tae-moo recognizes her despite her disguise, but he uses this knowledge to his advantage. Rather than being deterred, Tae-moo becomes fascinated by Ha-ri's boldness and initiative. He proposes a business arrangement: he will help Ha-ri avoid arranged marriages if she agrees to be his fake girlfriend. What begins as a calculated arrangement gradually transforms into genuine feelings as the two navigate their complicated dynamic.

The beauty of this plot lies not just in the central romance but in how it unfolds with multiple layers of complexity. There are workplace complications, family expectations, rival love interests, and the constant tension of keeping up appearances while actual feelings develop. The drama does an excellent job of balancing comedic mishaps with genuinely touching moments, ensuring that viewers remain invested in both the humor and the emotional journey of the characters.

Cast and Characters

  • Ahn Hyo-seop as Kang Tae-moo - The enigmatic and powerful CEO of the company serves as one half of the central romantic pairing. Tae-moo initially appears to be a cold, calculating businessman who is focused entirely on his career and company expansion. However, Ahn Hyo-seop's nuanced performance reveals layers beneath this exterior. His character undergoes a significant transformation throughout the series as he falls for Ha-ri, learning to embrace vulnerability and spontaneity. Ahn Hyo-seop brings a perfect combination of intensity, charm, and comedic timing to the role, making Tae-moo simultaneously intimidating and adorable.
  • Kim Se-jeong as Shin Ha-ri - The intelligent and resourceful female protagonist is an employee at Tae-moo's company who possesses both ambition and a quick wit. Ha-ri is relatable precisely because she is neither a damsel in distress nor an all-powerful heroine – she is simply a competent woman trying to navigate the pressures of society, family expectations, and her burgeoning feelings for her CEO. Kim Se-jeong brings remarkable energy to the role, balancing comedic scenes with moments of genuine vulnerability. Her chemistry with Ahn Hyo-seop is palpable and serves as the emotional foundation of the entire series.
  • Kim Min-gue as Cha Sung-hoon - A significant supporting character who adds depth to the narrative beyond the main romantic storyline. Sung-hoon is another member of the workplace who becomes entangled in the complexities of Ha-ri and Tae-moo's fake relationship. His character provides additional layers of conflict and emotional resonance as the story progresses, demonstrating that the supporting cast plays a crucial role in the drama's overall success.
  • Seol In-a as Jin Young-seo - A character who contributes to the romantic tension and complications within the narrative. Young-seo's presence adds another dimension to the story, creating situations that test the bonds between the main characters and challenge their evolving relationships. Seol In-a's performance ensures that this character feels fully realized rather than merely existing as an obstacle.
  • Lee Duck-hwa as Kang Da-goo - A supporting character who provides both comedic moments and emotional anchoring. Da-goo's interactions with other characters add warmth and humor to various scenes throughout the series, contributing to the overall charm of the ensemble cast.
  • Kim Kwang-kyu as Shin Joong-hae - Another supporting character whose presence enriches the workplace dynamics and family elements of the story. Kwang-kyu's performance helps ground the narrative in the real consequences and complications that arise from the various schemes and relationships depicted in the drama.

Why You Should Watch

Perfect Balance of Comedy and Genuine Emotion

Business Proposal excels at what many romantic comedies struggle to achieve: maintaining consistent laughs without sacrificing emotional depth. The humor ranges from slapstick comedy to witty banter and situational comedy that feels organic to the characters and their circumstances. A scene where Ha-ri attempts to act poorly on her blind date is comedy gold, yet mere episodes later, scenes where both characters must confront their genuine feelings for one another will leave you reaching for tissues. This tonal balance is achieved through intelligent writing that respects both the comedic and dramatic elements of the story. The writers understand that the best rom-coms are those where you're laughing one moment and genuinely moved the next, often in the same scene.

Exceptional Chemistry Between Lead Actors

The success of any romantic drama hinges on the chemistry between its lead actors, and Ahn Hyo-seop and Kim Se-jeong deliver in spades. Their interactions crackle with energy whether they're engaged in comedic banter, navigating workplace complications, or sharing quiet, intimate moments where their characters' true feelings shine through. There's a playfulness in their scenes together that makes their relationship feel authentic and worth rooting for. The progression from Ha-ri's nervousness around her CEO to genuine comfort and affection is portrayed beautifully, and you can see the exact moment when both characters realize their feelings are real. This chemistry elevates every scene they share, making the 12-episode run feel substantial and satisfying rather than rushed or dragged out.

Sharp Writing and Smart Humor

Beyond surface-level romantic tropes, Business Proposal offers intelligent observations about workplace dynamics, social pressures, family expectations, and the masks we wear in different contexts. The humor often stems from relatable situations – the awkwardness of workplace romance, the peculiarities of corporate hierarchies, the difficulty of maintaining a secret relationship. The writing is clever enough to make these situations funny without relying on mean-spirited jokes or humor at the expense of the characters. Instead, you find yourself laughing with the characters as they navigate complications together. The dialogue crackles with wit, particularly the verbal sparring between Ha-ri and Tae-moo, which showcases their intelligence and mutual respect despite the power imbalance in their workplace relationship.

Final Thoughts

Business Proposal stands as a testament to the enduring appeal of romantic comedy when executed with care, intelligence, and strong performances. The series doesn't reinvent the romantic comedy wheel, but it navigates familiar territory with such charm and wit that it feels fresh and engaging. The 8.428 TMDB rating represents not a perfect score, but rather a reflection of its strong execution across all elements – storytelling, acting, direction, and production. With just 12 episodes, Business Proposal respects viewers' time while delivering a complete, satisfying narrative arc. It's a series that leaves you smiling long after the final episode ends, the kind of feel-good entertainment that serves as a palate cleanser between heavier dramas or as the perfect comfort watch. If you've been on the fence about diving into this K-drama, the time to start is now. Business Proposal is waiting to charm you with its wit, humor, and surprisingly touching romance.
