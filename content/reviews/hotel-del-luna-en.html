Plot Summary

Hotel Del Luna presents an intriguing premise centered around an exclusive hotel that caters to an extraordinary clientele: deceased souls who have unfinished business in the mortal world. The story begins when Goo Chan-sung, an elite and ambitious hotelier with an impeccable track record in the hospitality industry, is unexpectedly invited to manage this mysterious establishment. His management skills and professional expertise seem perfectly suited for the position, but he quickly discovers that this hotel operates according to rules and realities far beyond his conventional understanding. The hotel's enigmatic ancient owner, Jang Man-wol, has presided over this supernatural establishment for over a century, maintaining her mysterious past and guarding secrets that gradually unfold throughout the series. As Chan-sung navigates his new role managing rooms for ghostly guests with various emotional burdens and unresolved issues, he becomes increasingly intrigued by Man-wol's true nature and the circumstances that bound her to the hotel for so long. Through their interactions and the countless guest stories they encounter, a complex relationship develops between the two protagonists, blending romance, mystery, and the deeper exploration of what it means to find peace and closure. The narrative deftly weaves together episodic guest stories with the developing romantic tension and the central mystery of Man-wol's past, creating a rich tapestry of emotional storytelling that rewards consistent viewership.
